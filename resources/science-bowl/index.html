How it works

A fast quiz-bowl match, played in pairs of questions

The National Science Bowl is run by the U.S. Department of Energy. Schools send teams of four or five students, and four play at a time. Regional winners advance to the National Finals in Washington, D.C.

1

Toss-up

The moderator reads a question and any player can buzz in. A correct answer scores 4 points. Teammates cannot talk during a toss-up.

2

Bonus

The team that wins the toss-up gets a bonus question worth 10 points, and now the whole team can confer before the captain answers.

3

Repeat, fast

Rounds move quickly, so speed, accuracy and knowing when to buzz decide close matches as much as raw knowledge does.
